## FAQ

### 1. Is this free?

Yes. AI Spend Auditor is free to use. You can run an audit without creating an account.

### 2. Do I need to create an account?

No. The audit does not require login or signup. Email is requested only after the report is shown, so you can save or receive a copy of the audit.

### 3. Are the savings guaranteed?

No. Savings are estimates based on user-provided inputs, public pricing references, and rule-based recommendations. Actual savings may vary based on billing cycle, discounts, contracts, usage patterns, taxes, and plan limits.

### 4. Do you use AI to calculate savings?

No. Savings calculations are handled by deterministic audit rules. AI is only used to generate a short human-readable summary after the audit result has already been calculated.

### 5. Is my report public?

Each audit gets a public shareable report URL, but private lead details such as email, company name, and role are not shown on the public page.

---

## Disclaimer

Savings are estimates based on user-provided inputs and public pricing references. Pricing may vary by region, billing cycle, taxes, usage, discounts, and enterprise agreements. The audit is designed to help teams identify potential optimization opportunities, not to guarantee cost reduction.
